# Banned tells

These are checked mechanically after writing (see `src/lib/build/gate.js`), and a failure triggers one targeted repair and then fails the job honestly.

- **No em dashes** and their look-alikes. Use a comma, a colon, brackets or a full stop.
- **No machine-voice phrases**, such as the stock openings, filler transitions and summarising clichés listed in the gate.
- Do not announce what you are about to do ("In this chapter we will explore").
- Do not end a chapter with a moral that restates it.
