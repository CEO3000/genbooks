# Prose style

- Write at a plain, readable level for the stated audience.
- Use active verbs and specific nouns. Cut adverbs that repeat the verb.
- Keep a steady pace: alternate explanation, example and short reflection.
- Address the reader directly where it helps, and stay consistent about it.
