# Cover design

Produce a front cover that fits the book's subject and audience.

- The title must be large, legible and correctly spelled. The author name sits below or above it.
- Keep the design simple: one clear image or motif and a limited colour palette.
- Avoid small text, busy backgrounds and recognisable real people.
- Leave space for the spine and back cover when a paperback is requested.
