# Non-fiction craft

- State the promise of the book early and keep delivering on it.
- Give each chapter one job. A reader should be able to say what it taught them.
- Prefer concrete examples, short scenes and practical steps to abstract statements.
- Define a term when you first use it. Do not assume knowledge the audience does not have.
- Vary sentence length. Keep paragraphs short enough to read comfortably on a phone.
- End each chapter with a short summary or a next step.
